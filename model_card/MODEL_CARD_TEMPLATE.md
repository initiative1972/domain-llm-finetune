## Intended use
- **Primary use:** answer employee questions about internal policies **using only the
  provided policy context**, cite the relevant policy id, and **refuse / escalate** when
  no provided policy covers the question.
- **Out of scope:** legal advice, decisions without retrieved context, any use on real
  personal/confidential data without appropriate controls.

## Limitations & honest boundaries
- Trained and evaluated on **synthetic data at single-GPU scale**; numbers are
  illustrative of the *method*, not production performance.
- Refusal/citation metrics are **heuristic** (regex + phrase match), not a human eval.
- No safety red-teaming or clinical/legal validation has been performed.

## Ethical considerations
- Grounded-answer-or-refuse design is intended to reduce hallucination and unsupported
  claims, but does **not** guarantee correctness; a human reviewer remains accountable.
- If adapted to real data, apply PII handling, access control, and audit logging.
